# Funding

MacGaming may accept voluntary donations and sponsorships to support development,
testing and maintenance. GPL-3.0-or-later allows commercial activity; financial
support does not change recipients' license rights or buy exclusive ownership.

No payment recipient, donation URL or sponsor account has been configured. The
example in `.github/FUNDING.yml.example` is deliberately inactive. When an approved
public account is available, copy it to `.github/FUNDING.yml` and fill in only the
providers actually used. No bank details or private identity documents belong in Git.

[GitHub Sponsors](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/getting-started-with-github-sponsors/about-github-sponsors)
has its own eligibility, account-verification and payment terms. Those are separate
from the source-code license. Payment-provider and tax obligations must be assessed
for the recipient's actual situation before collecting funds.

Donations are optional. They do not promise that a game will work, guarantee a fix,
or grant an endorsement by the authors of external dependencies. Sponsored work
should state deliverables explicitly and respect each dependency's license.
